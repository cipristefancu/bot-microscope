Step-by-Step Guide: How This Works (In Plain English) Updated Engine + Proof
Step 1 — Sampling & Strict No-Guess Policy

Taste one spoonful—and never invent missing Instagram numbers

Like tasting a stirred pot of soup rather than drinking it all, checking ~300 followers from a 120,000-follower audience estimates the true rate within ±5.6%. Under our strict R-NOFAKE rule, unopened accounts keep null stats—never fake placeholders. Passive hover cards only auto-clear as Clean if worst-case missing clues stay ≤34p, or count as Bot if known clues already beat 60p.

Step 2 — Split Forensics & Ratio Cap

100-Point Checklist (digits +20 vs. stitched +10 & 60p ratio ceiling)

Every checked account starts at 0 pts using publicly visible profile traits:

  • Identity & Activity: No photo (+20p), 0 posts (+25p) or 1–6 same-day setup posts (+15p), empty bio (+10p), follow-exchange 5K+/5K+ ring (+20p).
  • Split Handle Forensics: Dense digits / word.digits / 5+ digit run earn +20p; mutually exclusive stitched punctuation handles (no digits) earn only +10p.
  • Two-Independent-Witnesses Cap (max 60p): Follow-ratio signals (mass-follow +25, extreme 15×+ +30, low-audience +15, shared-post +20) cap together at 60p so one ratio trait alone cannot reach Bot (>60p).
  • Private Bots Audited Fairly: Private accounts still get scored on visible header counts, photo, bio, and handle—not given a free pass.
  • Manual Priority: A human verdict always overrides any heuristic score.
Step 3 — 3-Tier Verdicts (100% Partition)

🔴 Bots (>60p) + 🟡 Suspicious (35–60p) + 🟢 Clean (0–34p) = 100%

Clean (0–34p): Quiet real fans pass safely (or any account you confirm Real). Suspicious (35–60p): Grey-zone profiles (such as 0 posts + empty bio = 35p, or high-ratio lurkers hitting the 60p cap) are flagged amber for a human eye check instead of being mislabeled. Bot (>60p or Confirmed Bot): Requires >60p from independent witnesses—and your manual verdict always overrides the auto-score.

Step 4 — Gen-1 Farms vs. Gen-2 Bots

What math catches automatically vs. what the human eye spots in 3 seconds

Generation-1 Farms leave mathematical footprints (>60p: missing photo, empty bio, extreme follow ratios, digit-farm handles). Generation-2 Maintained Bots look polished on paper—real photo, curated feed posts, AI/copied bio, normal ratios—scoring 0–60p. No header rule alone should accuse those accounts blindly; human review catches artificial curation in 3 seconds, turning auto >60p into an honest verified floor while manual labels capture disguised Gen-2 bots.

Step 5 — Locked Out-of-Sample Proof

Tested on 1,417 unseen complete labels & live organic control accounts

On our locked out-of-sample test set (N=1,417 trusted complete profiles out of 5,203 total labels: 865 bots, 552 humans): auto >60p achieved 97.7% Precision [96.0%, 98.6%] (543/556) and 97.6% Specificity (539/552), catching 62.8% (543/865) of all bots as the conservative auto floor. In the 35–60p Suspicious band, 62.0% (127 vs. 78) were real bots. On live organic control creators: @freestate.ai 0.0% (0/44), plus two independent organic creators at 0.0% auto (1/22 manual) and 2.1% (1/48) auto-bots.

Step 6 — The #1 Honesty Check

“Are you 100% sure? Can you prove every number?”

Yes, we can defend every flagged account’s exact clues: public Instagram header stats, clue weights, 95% Wilson error bounds, and every human verification click are auditable row by row. Equally honest about limits: heuristic scores prove observable profile patterns—not private transactions, bought packages, or account intent. We pair a 97.7%-precise auto floor with a transparent Suspicious review queue instead of overclaiming.
